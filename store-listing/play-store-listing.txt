Language: English (en-US)

App name:
Fort: Gym Routines & Tracker

Short description:
Create gym routines, log every set and see your progress. All on your phone.

Full description:
Fort is a gym tracker built around reusable routines. Create a routine once and start it every time you train: Fort logs each set, times your rests, shows what you did last time and charts how you progress.

ROUTINES
• Build your routines from a catalog of more than 300 exercises, each with its muscles, equipment, description and illustration.
• Search exercises and filter them by muscle or type.
• Add your own exercises when one is missing.

WORKOUTS
• Log weight and reps, reps only, time or distance, depending on the exercise.
• See what you did last time next to each set.
• Rest timer after each set, and a timer for the whole workout.
• Add extra exercises on the fly without changing the routine.
• A notification shows the workout in progress with its timer.
• A summary of everything you did when you finish.

PROGRESS
• A chart for each exercise: estimated one-rep max, best set, best time or speed, over 4 weeks, 3 months, 6 months or a year.
• Body weight log with its chart, a weekly weigh-in reminder and your body mass index.
• History of every day you trained: correct past workouts or log one you forgot to record.
• Your athlete type, your consistency and an activity calendar.

YOUR DATA STAYS ON YOUR PHONE
• No account, no ads and no analytics. Nothing is sent over the internet.
• Export your data to a file and import it on another phone.

MADE YOUR WAY
• Light and dark themes, and six accent colors.
• Kilograms or pounds, kilometers or miles.
• Available in English, Spanish, Catalan, Basque, French and Portuguese.

Fort is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease.
